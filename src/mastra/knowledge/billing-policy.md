# SupportFlow — Billing & Refund Policy

## Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Enterprise customers can pay via invoice with NET-30 terms. All prices are in USD.

## Billing Cycle
All plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual plans receive a 20% discount. Billing occurs on the same day each month (or year) as the original subscription date.

## Refund Policy

### Eligibility
- Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the charge.
- Refunds are available for the current billing period only.
- Prorated refunds are not available for partial months.

### Refund Amounts
- **Monthly plans**: Full refund of the current month's charge if requested within 14 days.
- **Annual plans**: Full refund if requested within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, prorated refund for remaining full months.
- **Maximum automatic refund**: $500. Refunds exceeding $500 require manager approval and take 5-7 business days.

### How to Request a Refund
1. Go to Settings > Billing > Billing History.
2. Click "Request Refund" next to the relevant charge.
3. Select a reason for the refund.
4. Submit the request.
5. Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days to the original payment method.

### Non-Refundable Items
- Setup fees for Enterprise plans
- Custom integration development fees
- Data migration services
- Third-party add-on charges

## Plan Changes

### Upgrades
Upgrades take effect immediately. You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle.

### Downgrades
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. You retain access to all current features until then. Data and settings are preserved, but features exclusive to higher plans will become unavailable.

### Cancellation
You can cancel your subscription at any time in Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation:
- You retain read-only access for 30 days.
- After 30 days, your account and data are scheduled for deletion.
- Data is permanently deleted 60 days after cancellation.

## Invoices & Receipts
Invoices are automatically generated and sent to the account owner's email. You can also download them from Settings > Billing > Billing History. Enterprise customers with NET-30 terms receive invoices at the start of the billing period.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails:
1. We'll retry the charge after 3 days.
2. If the retry fails, we'll send an email notification.
3. After 7 days without successful payment, your account is downgraded to the free tier.
4. After 30 days, your account is suspended.
5. After 60 days, data is scheduled for deletion.

## Taxes
Prices do not include applicable taxes. Sales tax, VAT, or GST will be added based on your billing address and local regulations. Enterprise customers can provide tax-exempt certificates.
