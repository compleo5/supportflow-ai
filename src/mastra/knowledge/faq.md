# SupportFlow — Frequently Asked Questions

## Account & Login

### How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter your email address and we'll send a reset link within 5 minutes. The link expires after 24 hours. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

### How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?
Navigate to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. You can use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy) or SMS verification. We recommend using an authenticator app for stronger security.

### Can I change my email address?
Yes. Go to Settings > Account > Email Address. You'll need to verify the new email before the change takes effect. Note that login credentials will update to use the new email.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, including projects, files, and billing history, will be permanently removed after a 30-day grace period.

### Why is my account locked?
Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts within 15 minutes. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or use the "Forgot Password" flow to reset your credentials. If you believe your account was compromised, contact support immediately.

## Plans & Features

### What plans are available?
We offer three plans:
- **Starter** ($29/month): Up to 5 team members, 10GB storage, email support, basic analytics.
- **Professional** ($79/month): Up to 25 team members, 100GB storage, priority email and chat support, advanced analytics, API access.
- **Enterprise** (custom pricing): Unlimited team members, unlimited storage, dedicated account manager, SSO/SAML, SLA guarantee, custom integrations.

### How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. You won't lose data when downgrading, but some features may become unavailable.

### Is there a free trial?
Yes, all new accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan. No credit card required. At the end of the trial, you can choose a plan or your account will revert to a limited free tier.

### What happens when I exceed my storage limit?
You'll receive email warnings at 80% and 95% usage. Once you hit 100%, you can still access existing files but cannot upload new ones. Upgrade your plan or delete files to free up space.

## Integrations

### Which integrations are supported?
We support integrations with: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, and Webhooks. Enterprise plans also support custom integrations via our REST API.

### How do I connect Slack?
Go to Settings > Integrations > Slack. Click "Connect" and authorize the Slack workspace. You can then configure which channels receive notifications and which events trigger them.

### Can I use the API without an Enterprise plan?
API access is available on Professional and Enterprise plans. The Professional plan includes rate limits of 1,000 requests per hour. Enterprise plans have configurable rate limits.

## Data & Privacy

### Where is my data stored?
All data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers in the US (us-east-1) and EU (eu-west-1). You can choose your data region during account setup. Enterprise customers can request specific data residency configurations.

### Do you support data export?
Yes. Go to Settings > Account > Export Data. You can export all your data in JSON or CSV format. The export typically completes within 1 hour and you'll receive a download link via email.

### What is your data retention policy?
Active account data is retained for the lifetime of the account. After account deletion, data is permanently removed within 30 days. Billing records are retained for 7 years for compliance purposes.
